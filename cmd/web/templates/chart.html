Indicator glossary指標說明

BOLL
Bollinger Bands 20/2 — 20-bar SMA (mid) ± 2σ.

Direction: upper touch = stretched → mean-revert SHORT candidate (needs bearish confluence e.g. RSI overbought / bear div). Lower touch = mean-revert LONG candidate. Close outside the band = trend expansion, do NOT fade. Narrow band width (squeeze) = compression before an expansion; direction of the eventual break tells you the side.

布林通道 20/2 — 20 期均線 ± 2 標準差。

方向解讀：上軌觸及 = 過度拉伸，若伴隨 RSI 超買或頂背離可考慮均值回歸做空；下軌觸及反之考慮做多。收盤在外 = 趨勢延續，不可逆勢。窄軌（壓縮） = 波動即將擴張，破位方向決定操作邊。

VOL
Volume histogram — colored by candle direction (green up, red down).

Direction: price rising with expanding volume = healthy trend continuation. Price making new high but volume shrinking = distribution warning (likely top ahead). Green volume clusters > red = buyer pressure; opposite = seller pressure.

成交量柱狀 — 依 K 棒方向著色（綠漲紅跌）。

方向解讀：價漲量增 = 健康趨勢延續。新高但量縮 = 分批出貨，警惕頂部。綠量密集 > 紅量 = 買方壓力占優，反之則賣方主導。

VP
Visible-Range Volume Profile (VRVP) — per-level buy/sell volume in the visible window; recomputes on pan/zoom.

Colors are candle-direction approximations, NOT order-book flow:
• ■ Blue = green-candle (close ≥ open) volume — buyer-led bars at that price
• ■ Orange = red-candle volume — seller-led bars

Levels:
• POC (highest-volume row) — magnet; price tends to revert
• HVN — support below current price / resistance above
• LVN — air gap; price moves fast through

Reading combinations:
• Blue-heavy at HIGH prices + orange-heavy at LOW prices → trend-up structure: buyers stepping up on rallies, sellers exhausted on dips. Blue HVN below current price often becomes support.
• Orange-heavy at HIGH + blue-heavy at LOW → mirror bearish structure. Orange HVN above becomes resistance.
• Blue > orange at a specific level = absorption there → likely reversal from that side.
Caveat: if the window covers a long one-directional move, colors just mirror the trajectory itself — not "active pressure". Cross-check with recent price action.

可視範圍成交量分佈 (VRVP) — 視窗內各價位的買賣量，隨移動/縮放重新計算。

顏色是 K 棒方向的近似，不是訂單簿真正買賣流向：
• ■ 藍色 = 綠 K（收 ≥ 開）的成交量 — 該價位是買方主導成交
• ■ 橘色 = 紅 K 的成交量 — 該價位是賣方主導成交

三個關鍵位置：
• POC（最大量價位）— 磁吸點，價格常回歸
• HVN（高量區）— 現價下方為支撐、上方為壓力
• LVN（低量區）— 真空帶，價格快速穿越

顏色分佈組合解讀：
• 高價區藍多、低價區橘多 → 多頭結構：買方在高位承接、賣方在低位力竭。下方藍色 HVN 常成為支撐。
• 高價區橘多、低價區藍多 → 空頭結構，上方橘色 HVN 成為壓力。
• 某個價位藍色 > 橘色 = 該價位有吸籌 → 可能自此反轉。
陷阱：若視窗涵蓋長段單向走勢，顏色只是反映走勢軌跡本身，不代表「主動壓力」。要結合近期價格行為交叉驗證。

POC migration
Three POC price lines across nested windows — POC50 (last 50 bars, brightest), POC100 (medium), POC200 (long, faded purple). The label on POC50 shows an arrow + drift % vs POC200 and a `[stacked]` tag when the three are strictly monotonic.

Reading:
• Rising + stacked (↗ POC50 > POC100 > POC200) — volume-weighted uptrend regime → trade-with-trend LONG, favor pullback longs to the ladder.
• Falling + stacked (↘) → mirror → SHORT bias, fade rallies into the ladder.
• Flat / non-stacked — range regime; VP-driven mean-reversion trades score better than trend-continuation.
Drift < 0.5% collapses to "flat". Stacked = high-conviction regime; non-stacked drift = weaker signal.

三條 POC 水平線（不同視窗） — POC50（最近 50 根、最亮）、POC100（中）、POC200（長、褪色紫）。POC50 標籤帶方向箭頭 + 相對 POC200 的漂移 %，三線嚴格單調時加 [stacked]。

方向解讀：
• 上行且 stacked (↗ POC50 > POC100 > POC200) — 量能加權多頭趨勢 → 順勢做多，優先回踩到梯線做多。
• 下行且 stacked (↘) → 反之 → 做空偏好，反彈到梯線做空。
• Flat 或非 stacked — 盤整期，做均值回歸型別（VP 反轉）比做趨勢延續好。
漂移 < 0.5% 判為 flat。Stacked = 高信心的趨勢期；非 stacked 的漂移 = 弱訊號。

plan
Current engine plan — entry (orange), stop (red), TP1 (green), TP2 (teal) drawn as price lines.

Direction: not a signal by itself. Cross-check with structure (HVN below entry for longs, above for shorts) and with score/verdict. The line spacing tells you the R:R the engine is offering.

引擎當前計畫 — 入場（橘）、止損（紅）、TP1（綠）、TP2（青綠）水平線。

方向解讀：單獨不成訊號。需結合結構（多單看下方 HVN 支撐，空單看上方壓力）與評分驗證。線距代表引擎給出的盈虧比。

positions
Your open positions on this symbol — sourced from journal.csv. Each open trade draws entry / stop / TP1 / TP2 as price lines, prefixed with #ID L/S so multiple concurrent positions stay distinguishable.

Reading: LONG entry line is blue, SHORT is red; stop/TP colors match the plan layer. The entry line label shows live unrealized R (+0.42R etc.) refreshed every 5s against mark price. Externally-opened positions won't show up until you record them here (via the + price marker → Record Trade, or /journal/new).

當前開倉部位 — 由 journal.csv 提供。每筆開倉繪製入場 / 止損 / TP1 / TP2 水平線，前綴 #ID L/S 以區分多筆並存部位。

解讀：多單入場藍色、空單紅色；止損 / TP 顏色與 plan 一致。入場線標籤顯示即時未實現 R 值（+0.42R 等），每 5 秒依標記價更新。若在 BingX 外部開倉未記錄於此，圖上不會顯示 — 需透過 + 標記或 /journal/new 補記。

sweep
Liquidity sweep + reclaim — wick pierced a prior swing then closed back inside.

Direction: ▲ at swing-low = stops taken below → LONG bias (stop just under the wick's low). ▼ at swing-high = SHORT bias (stop above the wick). Failure mode: if the next bar closes back through the swing, the "sweep" was actually a genuine break — flip the thesis.

掃流動性 + 回收 — 影線刺破前高/低但收回內部。

方向解讀：▲ 掃低回收 = 洗掉多單止損後反轉 →做多偏好（止損置於影線最低下方）。▼ 掃高回收 =做空偏好（止損置於影線最高上方）。失敗訊號：下一根若再度收破前高/低，即為真突破，需反向操作。

流動性 EQH/EQL
Liquidity pools — clusters of equal highs (EQH, red) / equal lows (EQL, green) where stop orders rest. The label shows touch count (e.g. EQL ·4x).

Direction: pools are magnets — price is drawn to them, then often reverses. EQH above = buy-side liquidity → resistance / a magnet overhead. EQL below = sell-side liquidity → support. Old EQL sitting ABOVE price (lows made during a prior decline) flips to resistance on the retest — that's why you see green lines overhead after a drop. Role depends on where price is NOW, not the EQH/EQL label. Entry: a sweep-and-reject of a pool = fade it (the sweep-reject strategy). Failure: a decisive close THROUGH the pool = liquidity genuinely taken → continuation, not reversal.

The Nx = touch count — how many swing points cluster to form the pool (same-side swings within 0.15%). Each touch = another time price reacted there = more resting stops/orders piled up. That's how you judge a REAL wall: ≥5× = a wall (heavily defended), 3–4× = moderate, 2× = weak / barely a level. Also weigh band tightness (narrow = precise), recency (fresh > days-old), and confluence (pools + opens stacked at one price = stronger). ⚠️ A 24h box-top / recent high is NOT a pool — it's just the highest high, with no accumulated liquidity; don't treat it as a wall unless a multi-touch pool sits there.

流動性池 — 等高（EQH，紅）/ 等低（EQL，綠）聚集處，止損單囤積的地方。標籤顯示觸及次數（如 EQL ·4x）。

方向解讀：池子是磁鐵 — 價格被吸過去，然後常反轉。上方 EQH = 買方流動性 → 壓力 / 頭上磁鐵。下方 EQL = 賣方流動性 → 支撐。舊 EQL 跑到現價上方（下跌途中做的低）回測時翻成壓力。角色看它相對現價在上或下，不是只看 EQH/EQL 標籤。進場：掃池後反收 = 反向 fade（就是 sweep-reject 策略）。失敗訊號：果斷收盤穿過池子 = 流動性真的被吃掉 → 續勢,不是反轉。

Nx = 觸及次數 — 有幾個 swing 點群聚成這個池（同側、0.15% 內）。每一次觸及 = 價格在此反應一次 = 更多止損/掛單囤積。這就是判斷「真牆」的方法：≥5× = 真牆（重兵防守）、3–4× = 中等、2× = 弱 / 幾乎不算。再加權：帶寬（窄=精準）、新舊（近期>幾天前）、匯流（池+開盤價擠一起=更強）。⚠️ 24h 箱頂 / 近期高點「不是」池 — 那只是最高點、沒有囤積流動性；除非那裡剛好有多觸及的池，否則別當牆。

樞紐帶 S/R
Swing shelves — a price band that swing HIGHS and LOWS both cluster into (within 0.15%), i.e. a level price has pivoted around from both directions. Label reads H2/L4 · 258 bars: two highs, four lows, and the span from first touch to last.

How this differs from EQH/EQL above — the whole point: a liquidity pool is same-side (N highs = resting stops above = a sweep TARGET). A shelf is two-sided = price has flipped across it from support to resistance and back = genuine S/R. Five highs at one price and a genuinely two-sided level are indistinguishable to a same-side clusterer, and they behave differently. If both layers light up at the same price, that is the strongest configuration on the chart.

Reading the strength: the band is drawn solid and denser when min(highs, lows) ≥ 3 — pivoted around from both sides repeatedly. Dashed = ordinary. Judge by two-sidedness, not total touches: H2/L2 is a truer shelf than H5/L1, which is mostly a pool wearing one low — treat that one closer to an EQH. Span separates a level that has mattered across 300 bars from five touches inside one stretch of chop; it is counted in bars of the LOADED window, so it changes with timeframe.

Direction: unlike EQH/EQL a shelf has no inherent side — its role is decided by where price is NOW. Above it = support; below it = resistance. Stops: a shelf is something a stop belongs beyond, never inside — the band IS the noise range by definition, so a stop within it is a stop that ordinary movement reaches. Targets: a shelf in the profit direction is a place the move can stall, which makes it a take-profit candidate and, simultaneously, an obstacle to anything past it.

Reading SEVERAL bands together — the part that changes decisions: look at the GAPS, not the bands. Bands 20–70 points apart are one thick value area, where the market actually spent its time; if the level you are on fails, that is the magnet. A gap of >0.5% with no band in it is a vacuum — price crossed it fast and left no structure, so nothing is there to catch a fall. BTC 1h on 2026-09-07 showed both at once: five bands packed into 854 points (77,349–78,204), then a 1,443-point vacuum, then one lone band at 79,647–79,739. An isolated band above a vacuum and a band inside a cluster carry completely different risk — break the first and the next structure is 1,443 points away; break the second and the next is 20 points away. Same label, same touch count, not the same trade.

Where the pools sit relative to the band is the other half. Four configurations, three of them visible on BTC simultaneously that morning: (a) pool top == band bottom (EQL 4x topping out at exactly 79,647.0, the band floor) — the lows that BUILT the floor ARE the pool, so a break down instantly triggers resting stops: a break down has fuel. (b) pool bottom inside the band (EQH 6x starting at 79,722.7, in a band topping at 79,739) — the ceiling is not a wall, it is the FLOOR of a pool above. (c) a pool entirely inside the band (EQL 2x at 79,676.9–79,708.3) — weaker than its touch count says, because the band existing proves price already crossed that level from both sides. (d) no pool at either edge = pure structure, no liquidity trigger: quieter, likelier to hold. Note what each layer gives you: the pool layer merged that whole area into one 670-point contested zone, the shelf named the 92-point pivot inside it. Range from one, price from the other.

⚠️ "The next turn will happen in the band" is NOT what this shows. A shelf is where turns HAVE happened — that is its definition, so it is true by construction and predicts nothing. It is why a stop belongs beyond the band (the band is the observed turn-scatter, so a stop inside sits where price is known to travel), and that argument needs no forecast. The forecast itself is untested: on 2026-09-07 at 10:14 BTC turned at 79,756.1 — 17 points ABOVE the band top, inside a 6-touch EQH pool the band had nothing to do with. Place stops and read context with it; as an entry signal it has to clear cmd/gate first.

⚠️ You are seeing a selection. At most 6 bands within 5% of price, ranked. Raw detection finds ~15, including bands 20% away with a 16-bar span, and drawing all of them loses the two that matter. ⚠️ Descriptive, not predictive. This layer has never been A/B'd as a signal — it draws and votes nothing, unlike sweep-reject. Use it to place stops and read context, not as a reason to take a trade.

樞紐帶 — 一個高點和低點都聚在裡面的價帶（0.15% 內），也就是價格從兩個方向都在它身上轉過的位置。標籤 H2/L4 · 258 bars 讀法：2 個高點、4 個低點、第一次到最後一次觸及跨了 258 根 K。

跟上面 EQH/EQL 的差別 —— 這是重點：流動性池是單側的（N 個高點 = 上方囤積止損 = 掃單「目標」）。樞紐帶是雙側的 = 價格在它身上從支撐翻成壓力又翻回來 = 真正的支撐壓力。同一個價位的 5 個高點、跟一個真雙側的樞紐，對「只看同側」的偵測器來說長得一樣，但行為不同。兩個圖層在同一個價位同時亮起 = 圖上最強的組合。

強度怎麼看：min(高點, 低點) ≥ 3 時畫成實線、底色較濃 —— 代表兩邊都反覆轉過。虛線 = 一般。判斷要看兩面性，不是總觸數：H2/L2 比 H5/L1 更像真樞紐，後者其實是「一個池子配一個低點」，該當 EQH 看。span 用來區分「跨 300 根都在管事」和「一段盤整裡連續碰五次」；它是目前載入視窗內的 K 根數，換 TF 會變。

方向解讀：跟 EQH/EQL 不同，樞紐帶沒有天生的方向 —— 角色由現價在它上面還下面決定。在它上方 = 支撐；在它下方 = 壓力。停損：停損要放在樞紐帶外面，絕不放帶內 —— 那個帶「就是」雜訊範圍的定義，停損放裡面等於放在正常波動摸得到的地方。目標：獲利方向上的樞紐帶是行情可能停下來的位置，所以它同時是停利候選和它後面任何目標的障礙。

好幾條帶一起看 —— 這是真正會改變決策的部分：看間距，不要一條一條看。帶跟帶差 20–70 點的，那其實是一整塊價值區，市場真正待過的地方；現在這一層失守，那裡就是磁鐵。間距超過 0.5% 而中間沒有帶的，是真空 —— 價格穿過去太快、沒留下結構，所以掉下去沒東西接。BTC 1h 在 2026-09-07 同時出現兩者：五條帶擠在 854 點內（77,349–78,204），然後1,443 點的真空，然後一條孤立帶在 79,647–79,739。「真空上方的孤立帶」和「密集群裡的帶」風險完全不同 —— 前者破了，下一個結構在 1,443 點外；後者破了，下一個在 20 點外。標籤一樣、觸數一樣，不是同一筆交易。

池子相對於帶的位置是另外一半。四種配置，那天早上 BTC 同時展示了三種：(a) 池頂 == 帶底（EQL 4觸 的頂剛好是 79,647.0，也就是帶底）—— 築成帶底的那些低點就是那個池，所以跌破的瞬間就啟動止損：往下破有燃料。(b) 池底在帶內（EQH 6觸 從 79,722.7 起，而帶頂是 79,739）—— 帶頂不是牆，它是上方一個池子的地板。(c) 池子完全在帶內（EQL 2觸 79,676.9–79,708.3）—— 那個池比它的觸數看起來更弱，因為帶的存在本身就證明價格已經從兩邊穿過那裡了。(d) 兩邊都沒有池 = 純結構、沒有流動性觸發器：比較安靜、比較容易守住。另外注意兩層各給你什麼：池子那層把整片合併成一條 670 點的爭奪區，樞紐帶在裡面指出 92 點的轉軸。一個給範圍，一個給價位。

⚠️ 「下一個拐點會發生在帶裡面」不是這個圖層在講的事。樞紐帶是「拐點曾經發生在這裡」—— 那是它的定義，所以一定成立、也因此什麼都沒預測。它的用處是停損該放帶外（帶就是已觀測到的轉折散布範圍，停損放裡面等於放在價格已知會來回的地方），而這個論證不需要任何預測。預測本身沒被驗證：2026-09-07 10:14 BTC 在 79,756.1 轉向 —— 比帶頂高 17 點，在一個跟這條帶無關的 6 觸 EQH 池裡面。拿它擺停損和讀盤面；想當進場訊號用，得先過 cmd/gate。

⚠️ 你看到的是「篩選後」的結果。最多 6 條、且在現價 5% 內，已排序。原始偵測會找出約 15 條，包含離現價 20%、span 只有 16 根的垃圾 —— 全畫上去會蓋掉真正重要的兩條。⚠️ 它是描述性的,不是預測性的。這個圖層從未被 A/B 驗證過當訊號用 —— 它只畫圖、不投票，跟 sweep-reject 不同。拿它擺停損和讀盤面，不要拿它當進場理由。

HVN 目標
Volume-node take-profit candidates — shown when the plan layer is on. Each dashed line reads HVN 4.7R /1: 4.7 multiples of the plan's own risk, with 1 node still in the way. Violet = clean (nothing in front of it). POC marks the single heaviest node.

The /N is the whole point. R on its own misleads: a node four levels out showing +10R is a WORSE trade than one at +4.7R in clear air — and R is the number that looks better. Every node in front is a place the move can end. So take the first clean target (violet, bold in the side strip) as the TP to pre-fill, and treat the high-R ones behind it as stretch goals, not plans.

Two notes that are readings, not missing data. clear air — no volume node ahead = there is nothing in the path at all; the move has no volume-node resistance in front of it. no unobstructed target = every candidate sits behind another one; that is a congested path, so expect stalls and consider a nearer TP than the plan's.

⚠️ Display only. The liquidity-TP A/B of 2026-08-27 was REJECTED — "aim at the obvious level" is not a free win in this system. These are suggestions for you to weigh, never a mechanical TP rule, and they do not change the plan's own TP1/TP2.

量能節點停利候選 — 開啟 plan 圖層時顯示。每條虛線讀法 HVN 4.7R /1：用這筆交易自己的風險算出來的 4.7 倍，而且路上還有 1 個節點擋著。紫色 = 淨空（前面沒東西）。POC 標的是最重的那個節點。

/N 才是重點。只看 R 會選錯：四個節點外的 +10R 比淨空的 +4.7R 更差 —— 而 R 正好是那個看起來比較好的數字。前面每一個節點都是行情可能結束的地方。所以拿第一個淨空目標（紫色、側邊欄粗體那個）當要填的 TP，後面高 R 的當「延伸目標」，不要當計畫。

兩句 note 是「讀」,不是沒資料。clear air — no volume node ahead = 路上完全沒東西，這個方向沒有任何量能節點阻擋。no unobstructed target = 每個候選都躲在別人後面，代表路徑擁塞，要預期停頓、並考慮比計畫更近的 TP。

⚠️ 只是顯示。2026-08-27 的 liquidity-TP A/B 被否決了 —— 在這套系統裡「瞄準明顯的價位」不是免費的。這些是給你權衡的建議,絕不是機械化的 TP 規則,也不會改動 plan 自己的 TP1/TP2。

開盤線
Session opens — daily (00:00 UTC, = Asia open), weekly (Mon 00:00 UTC), London (07:00 UTC), NY (13:00 UTC). Institutional anchors.

Direction: price above BOTH daily & weekly open = long bias; below both = short. Opens act as S/R (trade the bounce/reject) and as mean-reversion magnets (price far from an open tends to revert to it). A close reclaiming an open = character shift. Several opens clustered at one price = a stronger wall.

開盤價 — 日開（00:00 UTC，= 亞洲開）、週開（週一 00:00 UTC）、倫敦開（07:00 UTC）、紐約開（13:00 UTC）。機構錨點。

方向解讀：站上日開且週開 =做多偏好；都在下 =做空。開盤價當支撐/壓力（做反彈/拒絕）+ 回歸磁鐵（離太遠常回測）。收盤收復開盤價 = 性格轉變。多條開盤價擠一起 = 更強的牆。

N字 struct
N-structure — push → pullback → push (the N-leg). Zigzag labels each turning point HH/HL (bullish) or LH/LL (bearish).

BOS = close beyond the leg's terminal swing (continuation); CHoCH = close back through the leg's origin (reversal warning — pivot zone voided). 樞紐區 (0.5–0.705 retrace band) = trend-aligned entry zone; invalid = structural stop; target = 1:1 measured move. Playbook: price pulls into the same-direction pivot zone without a CHoCH → look for continuation; a close through invalid → this N is void. ⏳ = event / in-zone are computed off the unclosed bar (can repaint before close); swing points & pivot-zone levels are close-confirmed and fixed.

N 字結構 — 推動 → 回檔 → 再推動。zigzag 把每個折點標 HH/HL(多)或 LH/LL(空)。

BOS = 收盤突破 leg 終端 swing(續勢);CHoCH = 收盤跌破 leg 起點(反轉警訊,樞紐區作廢)。樞紐區(0.5–0.705 回檔帶)= 順勢進場區,invalid = 結構停損,target = 1:1 量測。實戰:價格回落進同向樞紐區且未 CHoCH → 找續勢;收破 invalid → 這個 N 作廢。⏳ = event / in-zone 是拿未收盤 bar 即時價算的,收盤前可能變動;swing 折點與樞紐區位階是收盤確認、不變。

2× top/bot
Double top / double bottom — two peaks/valleys at ≈ same price with a lower/higher middle. Marker sits on the second pivot.

Direction: double-top → SHORT bias after neckline break; stop above the higher peak. Double-bottom → LONG bias after neckline reclaim; stop below the lower valley. Failure mode: neckline reclaimed against the direction → pattern invalidated, exit immediately.

雙頂 / 雙底 — 兩個相近價位的峰/谷，中間有較低/較高的回撤。標記位於第二個轉折點。

方向解讀：雙頂 → 跌破頸線後做空，止損置於較高頂之上。雙底 → 突破頸線後做多，止損置於較低底之下。失敗訊號：頸線被反向奪回即為型態失效，立即出場。

RSI div
Regular RSI(14) divergence — price and momentum disagree.

Direction: bearish reg-div (higher price high + lower RSI high) → momentum weakening at highs → SHORT bias, but wait for a structural break (BOS or trendline) before entering. Bullish reg-div mirror → LONG bias. Failure mode: divergence can extend across many bars — don't front-run. Absent a break, it's just a warning, not a trigger.

常規 RSI(14) 背離 — 價格與動能不一致。

方向解讀：頂背離（價創新高、RSI 未創新高）= 高位動能衰竭 →做空偏好，但需等結構破位（BOS 或趨勢線）再進場。底背離反之 →做多偏好。失敗訊號：背離可延續多根 K 棒，切勿搶反轉。無結構破位僅為警訊，不是觸發訊號。

Delta div
Estimated CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta) divergence — price makes a new extreme but the cumulative buy-minus-sell delta doesn't. Approximated per bar as (2·(close-low)/(high-low) - 1) · volume since the free BingX endpoint doesn't expose per-trade buyer flags; on liquid perps this tracks real CVD closely enough for the divergence signal.

Direction:
• Δ- (above bar) = bearish divergence → SHORT bias. During a quick pump, price makes a higher high but CVD makes a lower high — aggressive buyers not stepping up, likely exhaustion.
• Δ+ (below bar) = bullish divergence → LONG bias. During a quick dump, price makes a lower low but CVD makes a higher low — sellers running out of ammo.
Best used at the very end of an impulsive move as a fade signal. Currently visual-only — not fed into the engine score until backtested.

估算 CVD（累計成交量差）背離 — 價創新高/低但買減賣的累計差沒跟上。每根 K 棒以 (2·(close-low)/(high-low) - 1) · volume 近似（BingX 免費端點不提供逐筆買賣方向），在主流合約上足以呈現真實 CVD 的背離。

方向解讀：
• Δ-（K 棒上方）= 頂背離 → 做空偏好。急拉時價創新高但 CVD 未創新高，主動買單接不上，動能衰竭。
• Δ+（K 棒下方）= 底背離 → 做多偏好。急殺時價創新低但 CVD 未創新低，賣單耗盡。
脈衝走勢末端最有效。目前僅供視覺參考，未納入引擎評分（需先回測驗證）。

vol×
Volume anomaly — bar with vol ≥ 3× 20-SMA AND range > 1.5× ATR. Signals a real reaction bar.

Direction: green close + high vol on breakout = accumulation / trend continuation LONG. Red close + high vol on breakdown = distribution / continuation SHORT. High vol with a small close body = absorption / reversal candidate — check WHERE the close sits (upper half → bullish rejection; lower → bearish).

成交量異常 — 該根量 ≥ 20MA × 3 且振幅 > ATR × 1.5，代表真實反應。

方向解讀：綠 K 大量突破 =吸籌 / 趨勢延續做多。紅 K 大量破位 =派發 / 延續做空。若量大但實體小 = 吸收/反轉徵兆，看收盤位於上半 =多方拒絕下跌，下半 =空方拒絕上漲。

BB touch/break
Bollinger interaction events — wick or close relative to the outer band.

Direction: Touch at upper (wick out, close in) → mean-rev SHORT candidate. Touch at lower → LONG candidate. Break (close outside) → trend continuation on that side; do NOT fade — the band is failing as resistance/support.

布林通道觸碰 / 突破 — 影線或收盤相對外軌的位置。

方向解讀：觸碰（影線出但收盤內）：上軌 → 均值回歸做空；下軌 → 做多。突破（收盤在外） → 趨勢延續，不可逆勢，因外軌已失守。

MACD pane
MACD (12/26/9) sub-pane — histogram (green when momentum is positive, red when negative), MACD line (blue), signal line (orange). Reserved band at the bottom of the chart.

Direction: histogram above zero + expanding = strong bullish momentum → LONG bias. Below zero + expanding down = strong bearish → SHORT. Blue line crossing above orange = bullish cross, below = bearish. Contracting histogram = fading trend, watch for reversal.

MACD (12/26/9) 子面板 — 柱狀圖（動能為正時綠色、負時紅色）、MACD 線（藍色）、訊號線（橘色），佔用圖表底部保留區。

方向解讀：柱狀在零軸上方且擴大 = 強勢多頭動能 → 做多偏好；下方且擴大 = 強勢空頭 → 做空。藍線上穿橘線 = 金叉，下穿 = 死叉。柱狀收窄 = 動能衰退，注意反轉。

MACD ×
MACD signal-line crossovers (12/26/9).

Direction: ▲ teal = bullish cross (MACD line moves above signal) → LONG bias. ▼ orange = bearish cross → SHORT bias. Strong when: cross happens ABOVE zero for bullish (already in uptrend, adding); histogram is expanding (widening gap between lines); confluence with sweep/BOS. Weak when: MACD & signal are very close (whipsaw risk); price in tight range; cross against the higher-TF trend.

MACD 訊號線交叉 (12/26/9)。

方向解讀：▲ 青綠 = 金叉（MACD 線上穿訊號線）→ 做多偏好。▼ 橘 = 死叉 → 做空偏好。強訊號：零軸上方金叉（趨勢已多，順勢加倉）、柱狀圖擴大（兩線差距拉開）、伴隨掃盤或結構破位。弱訊號：MACD 與訊號線過度靠近（易假交叉）、價格在窄幅盤整、與更高週期趨勢相反的交叉。死叉反之亦然。

⚑ flags
Engine's take-position moment — anchored to the last bar of a confirmed setup.

Direction: ▲ long flag = engine votes LONG; ▼ short flag = SHORT. Cross-check the header score/verdict — a flag with STRONG verdict is high-conviction; NEUTRAL/AVOID means the engine wants to enter but the surrounding evidence is thin.

引擎進場點 — 標記於確認訊號的最後一根 K 棒。

方向解讀：▲ 多單旗 = 引擎判定做多；▼ 空單旗 =做空。需結合頂部評分/裁決 — STRONG 為高信心進場；NEUTRAL/AVOID 表示引擎想進場但周邊佐證不足，建議跳過。
