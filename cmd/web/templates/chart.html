Indicator glossary指標說明

BOLL
Bollinger Bands 20/2 — 20-bar SMA (mid) ± 2σ.

Direction: upper touch = stretched → mean-revert SHORT candidate (needs bearish confluence e.g. RSI overbought / bear div). Lower touch = mean-revert LONG candidate. Close outside the band = trend expansion, do NOT fade. Narrow band width (squeeze) = compression before an expansion; direction of the eventual break tells you the side.

布林通道 20/2 — 20 期均線 ± 2 標準差。

方向解讀：上軌觸及 = 過度拉伸，若伴隨 RSI 超買或頂背離可考慮均值回歸做空；下軌觸及反之考慮做多。收盤在外 = 趨勢延續，不可逆勢。窄軌（壓縮） = 波動即將擴張，破位方向決定操作邊。

VOL
Volume histogram — colored by candle direction (green up, red down).

Direction: price rising with expanding volume = healthy trend continuation. Price making new high but volume shrinking = distribution warning (likely top ahead). Green volume clusters > red = buyer pressure; opposite = seller pressure.

成交量柱狀 — 依 K 棒方向著色（綠漲紅跌）。

方向解讀：價漲量增 = 健康趨勢延續。新高但量縮 = 分批出貨，警惕頂部。綠量密集 > 紅量 = 買方壓力占優，反之則賣方主導。

VP
Visible-Range Volume Profile (VRVP) — buy/sell volume across price levels in the visible window; recomputes on pan/zoom.

Direction: POC (highest-vol row) acts as a magnet — price often reverts there. HVN = strong support (below current price) / resistance (above). LVN = air gap, price moves fast through. Blue > orange on a level = absorption at that price → likely reversal from that side; reverse = distribution.

可視範圍成交量分佈 (VRVP) — 視窗內各價位的買賣量，隨移動/縮放重新計算。

方向解讀：POC（最大量價位）為磁吸點，價格常回歸此處。HVN（高量區） = 現價下方為支撐、上方為壓力。LVN（低量區） = 真空帶，快速穿越。藍色 > 橘色 = 該價位有吸籌，可能自此反轉做多；反之則有派發風險。

plan
Current engine plan — entry (orange), stop (red), TP1 (green), TP2 (teal) drawn as price lines.

Direction: not a signal by itself. Cross-check with structure (HVN below entry for longs, above for shorts) and with score/verdict. The line spacing tells you the R:R the engine is offering.

引擎當前計畫 — 入場（橘）、止損（紅）、TP1（綠）、TP2（青綠）水平線。

方向解讀：單獨不成訊號。需結合結構（多單看下方 HVN 支撐，空單看上方壓力）與評分驗證。線距代表引擎給出的盈虧比。

positions
Your open positions on this symbol — sourced from journal.csv. Each open trade draws entry / stop / TP1 / TP2 as price lines, prefixed with #ID L/S so multiple concurrent positions stay distinguishable.

Reading: LONG entry line is blue, SHORT is red; stop/TP colors match the plan layer. The entry line label shows live unrealized R (+0.42R etc.) refreshed every 5s against mark price. Externally-opened positions won't show up until you record them here (via the + price marker → Record Trade, or /journal/new).

當前開倉部位 — 由 journal.csv 提供。每筆開倉繪製入場 / 止損 / TP1 / TP2 水平線，前綴 #ID L/S 以區分多筆並存部位。

解讀：多單入場藍色、空單紅色；止損 / TP 顏色與 plan 一致。入場線標籤顯示即時未實現 R 值（+0.42R 等），每 5 秒依標記價更新。若在 BingX 外部開倉未記錄於此，圖上不會顯示 — 需透過 + 標記或 /journal/new 補記。

sweep
Liquidity sweep + reclaim — wick pierced a prior swing then closed back inside.

Direction: ▲ at swing-low = stops taken below → LONG bias (stop just under the wick's low). ▼ at swing-high = SHORT bias (stop above the wick). Failure mode: if the next bar closes back through the swing, the "sweep" was actually a genuine break — flip the thesis.

掃流動性 + 回收 — 影線刺破前高/低但收回內部。

方向解讀：▲ 掃低回收 = 洗掉多單止損後反轉 →做多偏好（止損置於影線最低下方）。▼ 掃高回收 =做空偏好（止損置於影線最高上方）。失敗訊號：下一根若再度收破前高/低，即為真突破，需反向操作。

2× top/bot
Double top / double bottom — two peaks/valleys at ≈ same price with a lower/higher middle. Marker sits on the second pivot.

Direction: double-top → SHORT bias after neckline break; stop above the higher peak. Double-bottom → LONG bias after neckline reclaim; stop below the lower valley. Failure mode: neckline reclaimed against the direction → pattern invalidated, exit immediately.

雙頂 / 雙底 — 兩個相近價位的峰/谷，中間有較低/較高的回撤。標記位於第二個轉折點。

方向解讀：雙頂 → 跌破頸線後做空，止損置於較高頂之上。雙底 → 突破頸線後做多，止損置於較低底之下。失敗訊號：頸線被反向奪回即為型態失效，立即出場。

RSI div
Regular RSI(14) divergence — price and momentum disagree.

Direction: bearish reg-div (higher price high + lower RSI high) → momentum weakening at highs → SHORT bias, but wait for a structural break (BOS or trendline) before entering. Bullish reg-div mirror → LONG bias. Failure mode: divergence can extend across many bars — don't front-run. Absent a break, it's just a warning, not a trigger.

常規 RSI(14) 背離 — 價格與動能不一致。

方向解讀：頂背離（價創新高、RSI 未創新高）= 高位動能衰竭 →做空偏好，但需等結構破位（BOS 或趨勢線）再進場。底背離反之 →做多偏好。失敗訊號：背離可延續多根 K 棒，切勿搶反轉。無結構破位僅為警訊，不是觸發訊號。

Delta div
Estimated CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta) divergence — price makes a new extreme but the cumulative buy-minus-sell delta doesn't. Approximated per bar as (2·(close-low)/(high-low) - 1) · volume since the free BingX endpoint doesn't expose per-trade buyer flags; on liquid perps this tracks real CVD closely enough for the divergence signal.

Direction:
• Δ- (above bar) = bearish divergence → SHORT bias. During a quick pump, price makes a higher high but CVD makes a lower high — aggressive buyers not stepping up, likely exhaustion.
• Δ+ (below bar) = bullish divergence → LONG bias. During a quick dump, price makes a lower low but CVD makes a higher low — sellers running out of ammo.
Best used at the very end of an impulsive move as a fade signal. Currently visual-only — not fed into the engine score until backtested.

估算 CVD（累計成交量差）背離 — 價創新高/低但買減賣的累計差沒跟上。每根 K 棒以 (2·(close-low)/(high-low) - 1) · volume 近似（BingX 免費端點不提供逐筆買賣方向），在主流合約上足以呈現真實 CVD 的背離。

方向解讀：
• Δ-（K 棒上方）= 頂背離 → 做空偏好。急拉時價創新高但 CVD 未創新高，主動買單接不上，動能衰竭。
• Δ+（K 棒下方）= 底背離 → 做多偏好。急殺時價創新低但 CVD 未創新低，賣單耗盡。
脈衝走勢末端最有效。目前僅供視覺參考，未納入引擎評分（需先回測驗證）。

vol×
Volume anomaly — bar with vol ≥ 3× 20-SMA AND range > 1.5× ATR. Signals a real reaction bar.

Direction: green close + high vol on breakout = accumulation / trend continuation LONG. Red close + high vol on breakdown = distribution / continuation SHORT. High vol with a small close body = absorption / reversal candidate — check WHERE the close sits (upper half → bullish rejection; lower → bearish).

成交量異常 — 該根量 ≥ 20MA × 3 且振幅 > ATR × 1.5，代表真實反應。

方向解讀：綠 K 大量突破 =吸籌 / 趨勢延續做多。紅 K 大量破位 =派發 / 延續做空。若量大但實體小 = 吸收/反轉徵兆，看收盤位於上半 =多方拒絕下跌，下半 =空方拒絕上漲。

BB touch/break
Bollinger interaction events — wick or close relative to the outer band.

Direction: Touch at upper (wick out, close in) → mean-rev SHORT candidate. Touch at lower → LONG candidate. Break (close outside) → trend continuation on that side; do NOT fade — the band is failing as resistance/support.

布林通道觸碰 / 突破 — 影線或收盤相對外軌的位置。

方向解讀：觸碰（影線出但收盤內）：上軌 → 均值回歸做空；下軌 → 做多。突破（收盤在外） → 趨勢延續，不可逆勢，因外軌已失守。

MACD pane
MACD (12/26/9) sub-pane — histogram (green when momentum is positive, red when negative), MACD line (blue), signal line (orange). Reserved band at the bottom of the chart.

Direction: histogram above zero + expanding = strong bullish momentum → LONG bias. Below zero + expanding down = strong bearish → SHORT. Blue line crossing above orange = bullish cross, below = bearish. Contracting histogram = fading trend, watch for reversal.

MACD (12/26/9) 子面板 — 柱狀圖（動能為正時綠色、負時紅色）、MACD 線（藍色）、訊號線（橘色），佔用圖表底部保留區。

方向解讀：柱狀在零軸上方且擴大 = 強勢多頭動能 → 做多偏好；下方且擴大 = 強勢空頭 → 做空。藍線上穿橘線 = 金叉，下穿 = 死叉。柱狀收窄 = 動能衰退，注意反轉。

MACD ×
MACD signal-line crossovers (12/26/9).

Direction: ▲ teal = bullish cross (MACD line moves above signal) → LONG bias. ▼ orange = bearish cross → SHORT bias. Strong when: cross happens ABOVE zero for bullish (already in uptrend, adding); histogram is expanding (widening gap between lines); confluence with sweep/BOS. Weak when: MACD & signal are very close (whipsaw risk); price in tight range; cross against the higher-TF trend.

MACD 訊號線交叉 (12/26/9)。

方向解讀：▲ 青綠 = 金叉（MACD 線上穿訊號線）→ 做多偏好。▼ 橘 = 死叉 → 做空偏好。強訊號：零軸上方金叉（趨勢已多，順勢加倉）、柱狀圖擴大（兩線差距拉開）、伴隨掃盤或結構破位。弱訊號：MACD 與訊號線過度靠近（易假交叉）、價格在窄幅盤整、與更高週期趨勢相反的交叉。死叉反之亦然。

⚑ flags
Engine's take-position moment — anchored to the last bar of a confirmed setup.

Direction: ▲ long flag = engine votes LONG; ▼ short flag = SHORT. Cross-check the header score/verdict — a flag with STRONG verdict is high-conviction; NEUTRAL/AVOID means the engine wants to enter but the surrounding evidence is thin.

引擎進場點 — 標記於確認訊號的最後一根 K 棒。

方向解讀：▲ 多單旗 = 引擎判定做多；▼ 空單旗 =做空。需結合頂部評分/裁決 — STRONG 為高信心進場；NEUTRAL/AVOID 表示引擎想進場但周邊佐證不足，建議跳過。
