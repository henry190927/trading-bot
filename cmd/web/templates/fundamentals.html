ⓘ what these mean欄位說明
  • Rating — buy = strong quality at a fair price · hold = own/keep, don't add · rich = great company but very expensive, trim / sell into strength · avoid = weak fundamentals (value-trap). NOT a trade signal — spot / buy-hold only.buy = 品質強且估值合理 · hold = 持有/續抱、別加碼 · rich = 好公司但太貴,減碼/逢高賣 · avoid = 基本面弱(價值陷阱)。非交易訊號 —— 純現貨。
  • Quality (0-100) — composite of growth · profitability · balance-sheet. ≥60 strong, <40 weak.成長·獲利·資產負債的綜合。≥60 強、<40 弱。
  • Valuation (0-100) — HIGHER = cheaper (P/E + P/S vs thresholds). ≥40 fair, low = expensive.越高越便宜(P/E+P/S)。≥40 合理,低=貴。
  • g/p/b — the three quality sub-scores: growth / profitability / balance-sheet.品質三子分:成長 / 獲利 / 資產負債。
  • P/E — price ÷ earnings. Lower = cheaper; very high = priced for growth. "—" = negative/no earnings.股價 ÷ 每股盈餘。越低越便宜;極高=市場定價成長。「—」=虧損/無盈餘。
  • P/S — price ÷ sales. Useful when earnings are thin/negative.股價 ÷ 營收。盈餘薄/負時好用。
  • Rev YoY — revenue growth year-over-year.營收年增率。
  • Net M — net profit margin — how much of revenue becomes profit.淨利率 —— 營收轉成獲利的比例。
  • D/E — debt ÷ equity. ≤1 healthy, >2 leveraged.負債 ÷ 權益。≤1 健康,>2 高槓桿。
  • Next ER — next earnings date (Nd = days away). Expect a volatility gap around it.下次財報日(Nd=天數)。前後易有跳空波動。
