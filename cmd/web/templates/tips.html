🎚️ Price pierced the BB? Reversal or trap depends on regime 價格溢軌破 BB?反轉還是陷阱,看 regime ▾
  1. Range / neutral regime → YES, fade it. A BB pierce = statistical overextension → high odds of snapping back to the mean. This is exactly the engine's BB-touch vote (and why MR wins on ETH). 盤整 / neutral regime → YES,可以 fade。 溢軌 = 統計上過度延伸 → 高機率彈回均值。這就是引擎「BB 觸帶」那一票在做的事(也是 MR 在 ETH 會贏的原因)。
  2. Strong trend → NO, it's a trap. Price walks the band — it rides the edge for many bars while the band expands. Fading the first pierce = standing in front of the train (why MR loses on SOL → we built StructMomentum to go WITH it). 強趨勢 → NO,是陷阱。 價格會沿著軌道走——貼著邊界跑很多根、布林還擴張。去 fade 第一次溢軌 = 站在火車前面(所以 MR 在 SOL 慘賠 → 我們才做 StructMomentum 順勢)。
  3. How to tell the regime: structure (neutral = fade OK; HH-HL / LH-LL trend = don't) + band width (flat/contracting = range; expanding = trend). 怎麼分辨 regime: 結構(neutral = 可 fade;HH-HL / LH-LL 趨勢 = 別 fade)+ 帶寬(平/縮 = 區間;擴張 = 趨勢)。
  4. BB alone is weak. The engine never votes on BB alone — it needs BB + RSI extreme + sweep + divergence confluence. Strongest fade = a pierce that immediately reclaims back inside the band (failed breakout), NOT price accelerating outside it (that's trend). 只靠 BB 太弱。 引擎從不單靠 BB —— 要 BB + RSI 極值 + sweep + 背離 共振。最強的 fade 是溢軌後立刻收回帶內(失敗突破),不是價格加速衝出帶外(那是趨勢)。

📐 In a trend the band edge is evidence of momentum, not a reversal signal. 📐 在趨勢裡,布林邊界是動能的證據,不是反轉的訊號。

  3. Fix: in a stacked / trending regime, take a market entry (accept a slightly worse price) or skip. Reserve pullback-limits for neutral / range regimes. 修法:stacked / 趨勢 regime 就 market 進(接受稍差價位)或跳過。回撤限價留給 neutral / range。

📦 Reading OI: it says how many, never whose 判讀 OI:它說「有多少」,從不說「是誰的」 ▾
  1. Every contract has a long AND a short. So open interest carries no direction on its own — it counts positions that EXIST. It moves only when one is opened or closed, never when one changes hands: both sides opening → OI up; both closing → OI down; one opening while the other closes → OI flat. OI says new-or-old; price says whose. 每一張合約必然有一個多方和一個空方。 所以 OI 本身不帶方向 —— 它數的是「存在的倉位」。它只在開倉或平倉時變動,換手不算:兩邊都開新倉 → OI 增;兩邊都平 → OI 減;一邊開一邊平 → OI 不變。OI 說「新舊」,價格說「是誰的」。
  2. The four quadrants. OI↑ + price↑ = new longs, the healthiest continuation up. OI↑ + price↓ = new shorts, the healthiest continuation down — and simultaneously squeeze fuel. OI↓ + price↑ = shorts covering, the rally is burning its fuel. OI↓ + price↓ = longs capitulating, the drop is burning its fuel. 四象限。 OI↑ + 價格↑ = 新多單,最健康的續漲。OI↑ + 價格↓ = 新空單,最健康的續跌 —— 同時是擠壓燃料。OI↓ + 價格↑ = 空單回補,漲勢在燒燃料。OI↓ + 價格↓ = 多單投降,跌勢在燒燃料。
  3. A falling-OI move is the weaker one. A rally on shrinking OI is shorts buying back — when they finish there is no bid left. A rally on growing OI is new money, and that can keep going. Same logic inverted for a sell-off. OI 減的行情比較弱。 上漲配 OI 減 = 空單在回補,補完就沒有買盤了。上漲配 OI 增 = 真的有新錢進來,那才能延續。下跌同理反過來。
  4. OI says how many, funding says who is paying. They only mean something together: OI↑ + price↓ + funding NEGATIVE = shorts are both numerous and paying to hold = textbook squeeze setup. OI↑ + price↓ + funding POSITIVE = shorts are building but longs still pay = far less crowded than it looks. Thresholds the engine uses: OI ±2%, funding ±0.05% crowded / ±0.10% extreme. OI 說「有多少」,funding 說「誰在付錢」。 兩個合起來才有意義:OI↑ + 價格↓ + funding 為負 = 空單又多又在付錢 = 教科書擠壓設定。OI↑ + 價格↓ + funding 為正 = 空單在建但多方還在付錢 = 沒有看起來那麼擠。引擎用的門檻:OI ±2%、funding ±0.05% 擁擠 / ±0.10% 極端。
  5. ⚠ Do NOT read a slope off the 5-minute samples. BingX republishes open interest roughly every 10 minutes: measured on our own store, 44% of consecutive 5-minute samples are byte-identical (112 of 254 on ETH). Those zeros are "the venue has not updated", not "nobody opened a position". Any "one bucket carried 54% of the build" read off raw 5-minute steps is measuring the publication cadence, not a whale. Dedupe repeated readings before computing any rate. ⚠ 不要用 5 分鐘取樣去讀斜率。 BingX 大約每 10 分鐘才重新發布一次 OI:量我們自己的 store,連續 5 分鐘樣本有 44% 是完全相同的數字(ETH 254 格裡 112 格)。那些「0」的意思是「交易所還沒更新」,不是「沒有人開倉」。任何從原始 5 分鐘級距讀出來的「某一格佔了 54% 的堆積量」都是在量發布頻率,不是在量巨鯨。要算任何速率之前,先把重複值去掉。

🕛 Session opens = institutional anchors: bias filter + S/R + magnet 開盤價 = 機構錨點:偏見過濾 + S/R + 回歸磁鐵 ▾
  1. Bias filter: price above BOTH daily & weekly open → long only; below both → short only. The open is the intraday/intraweek bull-bear divider — algos and desks anchor to it. 偏見過濾:站上日開且週開 → 只做多;都在下 → 只做空。開盤價是當日/當週的多空分界,算法跟機構都錨定它。
  2. S/R reaction: opens act as support/resistance — trade the bounce or rejection off them. A close back above/below an open = character shift (reclaim = bullish). S/R 反應:開盤價當支撐/壓力,做反彈或拒絕。收回站上/跌破開盤價 = 性格轉變(收復=轉多)。
  3. Mean-reversion magnet: when price extends far from an open, it tends to revert and test it — use the open as a TP or a fade reference, not a chase. 回歸磁鐵:價格離開盤價太遠 → 常回測它 → 拿開盤價當 TP 或 fade 參考,別追。
  4. Session framing + clusters: London (07:00 UTC) & NY (13:00 UTC) opens frame each session — watch the "open → sweep → reverse" killzone. When several opens cluster at one price, that's a stronger wall. 場次框架 + 群聚:倫敦(07:00 UTC)/紐約(13:00 UTC)開框各場次,盯「開盤→掃單→反轉」killzone。多條開盤價擠在同一價 = 更強的牆。

🎯 Read right ≠ traded well: location · size · breakeven 讀對盤 ≠ 做得好:點位 · size · 移保本 ▾
  1. Location — don't enter the MIDDLE of a box. A level can be real (a reclaim line / EQH edge) yet sit in no-man's-land between the base below and the wall above. Enter long there and both sides whipsaw you. 點位 —— 別進箱體中段。一個層級可以是真的(收回線 / EQH 頂緣),卻夾在「下方基底 ↔ 上方硬牆」的正中間。在那做多,兩面都洗你。
  2. Enter the EDGE: long a bounce off the base (support / weekly-daily open) OR a hold after breaking the wall — not the middle. If you're between two magnets, wait for one to resolve. 進邊緣:做多就進「基底反彈」(支撐 / 週開日開)或「突破牆後站穩」—— 不進中段。夾在兩個磁鐵之間,等其中一邊選完再進。
  3. Size so you can hold the plan. Oversized leverage (125× on a big notional) makes a 0.5% wiggle a big $ swing → 患得患失 → you break your own rules (don't cut when you should, don't move the stop). The read was right; the SIZE hijacked the execution. Right-size = calm = disciplined. Size 要讓你守得住計畫。過大槓桿(大名目 125×)讓 0.5% 波動變成大額跳動 → 患得患失 → 破自己的規則(該砍不砍、停損不移)。讀盤對,是 SIZE 綁架了執行。合理 size = 心態穩 = 守紀律。
  4. Failed first continuation → move stop to breakeven. Entry fills, price pokes up ONCE and fails — that failed re-test is the signal to lock breakeven. Then a later wick to your original stop just scratches instead of hurting. 首次續攻失敗 → 停損移保本。成交後戳上去「一次」就失敗 —— 那次失敗的再測就是移保本的訊號。之後插針到原停損只是平手,不會痛。
