🧭 Engine vs N-struct? Ask regime first Engine vs N-struct?先問 regime ▾
  1. Clear trend (HH-HL / LH-LL) or breakout → lean N-struct. The engine (mean-reversion) lags here. 趨勢明確(HH-HL / LH-LL)或突破 → 偏向 N struct。engine(均值回歸)在這裡會慢半拍。
  2. Neutral chop → lean engine. N-struct's pivot/CHoCH throws a lot of fakes. neutral 拉鋸 → 偏向 engine。N struct 的樞紐/CHoCH 假訊號多。
  3. Both agree → highest confidence (e.g. #11 engine TAKE + N-struct → +2.76R). 兩者同意 → 最高信心(例:#11 engine TAKE + N struct → +2.76R)。
  4. They clash → decide by regime + your own read. Don't let one indicator veto the other. 兩者衝突 → 用 regime + 你的讀盤裁決。別讓單一指標否決另一個。

🎣 Pullback limits won't fill in a trend — stop parking them 趨勢盤掛回撤限價會落空 — 別空等 ▾
  1. When drift is stacked (POC50>100>200) or price is trending, a pullback-limit entry often never fills — price runs without the retrace you waited for. 當 drift stacked(POC50>100>200)或價格在趨勢中,回撤限價常常填不到 — 價格沒回撤就直接噴走。
  2. Low-conviction plans (total 3, MOM 0) are the worst offenders — the engine's mean-reversion pullback rarely materializes in a drifting tape. 低信心 plan(total 3、MOM 0)最容易落空 — 均值回歸的回撤在 drift 盤很少真的發生。
  3. Fix: in a stacked / trending regime, take a market entry (accept a slightly worse price) or skip. Reserve pullback-limits for neutral / range regimes. 修法:stacked / 趨勢 regime 就 market 進(接受稍差價位)或跳過。回撤限價留給 neutral / range。

🛑 Don't fade a stacked regime — MR longs need range 別逆勢 fade stacked 盤 — MR 做多要 range ▾
  1. A mean-reversion long into a stacked-down regime gets run over — "price broke down, downtrend continued". The engine's MR edge assumes a range, not a trend. MR 做多逆一個 stacked-down regime 會被輾 —「broke down、downtrend continued」。MR 的邊際假設是 range,不是趨勢。
  2. Check regime first: stacked drift (POC ladder aligned) = trade with it; flat / non-stacked = mean-reversion is safe. 先看 regime:stacked drift(POC 梯對齊)= 順勢做;flat / non-stacked = 才適合回歸。
  3. If you must counter-trend, wait for a structural signal (BOS / CHoCH / sweep) confirming the turn — don't fade on RSI alone. 真要逆勢,等結構訊號(BOS / CHoCH / 掃單)確認轉折 — 別只靠 RSI 就 fade。

🛡️ Counter-signal against you? Close — don't wait for the stop 對手訊號出現?防禦性平倉 — 別等停損 ▾
  1. Holding a short while a double-bottom + RSI bull-div forms against you = ignoring the tape. That's a full −1R you could have cut. 抱空單卻讓 double bottom + RSI bull div 在對面成形 = 無視盤面。那是本可砍掉的 −1R。
  2. When structure flips against your position (opposite BOS/CHoCH, divergence, pivot reclaim), take the defensive exit — a give-back beats a full stop. 當結構對你的部位反轉(反向 BOS/CHoCH、背離、樞紐收復),防禦性出場 — 讓一點利也比吃滿停損好。
  3. This is +EV even when price later returns to target — you're paying for tail-risk insurance, not chasing perfection. 就算之後價格又回到 target,這仍是 +EV — 你買的是尾部風險保險,不是追求完美。
