🧭 Engine vs N-struct? Ask regime first Engine vs N-struct?先問 regime ▾
  1. Clear trend (HH-HL / LH-LL) or breakout → lean N-struct. The engine (mean-reversion) lags here. 趨勢明確(HH-HL / LH-LL)或突破 → 偏向 N struct。engine(均值回歸)在這裡會慢半拍。

  4. They clash → decide by regime + your own read. Don't let one indicator veto the other. 兩者衝突 → 用 regime + 你的讀盤裁決。別讓單一指標否決另一個。
