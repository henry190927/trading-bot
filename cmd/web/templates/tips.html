🧭 Engine vs N-struct? Ask regime first Engine vs N-struct?先問 regime ▾

  2. Neutral chop → lean engine. N-struct's pivot/CHoCH throws a lot of fakes. neutral 拉鋸 → 偏向 engine。N struct 的樞紐/CHoCH 假訊號多。

  4. They clash → decide by regime + your own read. Don't let one indicator veto the other. 兩者衝突 → 用 regime + 你的讀盤裁決。別讓單一指標否決另一個。

🎚️ Price pierced the BB? Reversal or trap depends on regime 價格溢軌破 BB?反轉還是陷阱,看 regime ▾
  1. Range / neutral regime → YES, fade it. A BB pierce = statistical overextension → high odds of snapping back to the mean. This is exactly the engine's BB-touch vote (and why MR wins on ETH). 盤整 / neutral regime → YES,可以 fade。 溢軌 = 統計上過度延伸 → 高機率彈回均值。這就是引擎「BB 觸帶」那一票在做的事(也是 MR 在 ETH 會贏的原因)。
  2. Strong trend → NO, it's a trap. Price walks the band — it rides the edge for many bars while the band expands. Fading the first pierce = standing in front of the train (why MR loses on SOL → we built StructMomentum to go WITH it). 強趨勢 → NO,是陷阱。 價格會沿著軌道走——貼著邊界跑很多根、布林還擴張。去 fade 第一次溢軌 = 站在火車前面(所以 MR 在 SOL 慘賠 → 我們才做 StructMomentum 順勢)。
  3. How to tell the regime: structure (neutral = fade OK; HH-HL / LH-LL trend = don't) + band width (flat/contracting = range; expanding = trend). 怎麼分辨 regime: 結構(neutral = 可 fade;HH-HL / LH-LL 趨勢 = 別 fade)+ 帶寬(平/縮 = 區間;擴張 = 趨勢)。
  4. BB alone is weak. The engine never votes on BB alone — it needs BB + RSI extreme + sweep + divergence confluence. Strongest fade = a pierce that immediately reclaims back inside the band (failed breakout), NOT price accelerating outside it (that's trend). 只靠 BB 太弱。 引擎從不單靠 BB —— 要 BB + RSI 極值 + sweep + 背離 共振。最強的 fade 是溢軌後立刻收回帶內(失敗突破),不是價格加速衝出帶外(那是趨勢)。

📐 In a trend the band edge is evidence of momentum, not a reversal signal. 📐 在趨勢裡,布林邊界是動能的證據,不是反轉的訊號。

🎣 Pullback limits won't fill in a trend — stop parking them 趨勢盤掛回撤限價會落空 — 別空等 ▾
  1. When drift is stacked (POC50>100>200) or price is trending, a pullback-limit entry often never fills — price runs without the retrace you waited for. 當 drift stacked(POC50>100>200)或價格在趨勢中,回撤限價常常填不到 — 價格沒回撤就直接噴走。
  2. Low-conviction plans (total 3, MOM 0) are the worst offenders — the engine's mean-reversion pullback rarely materializes in a drifting tape. 低信心 plan(total 3、MOM 0)最容易落空 — 均值回歸的回撤在 drift 盤很少真的發生。
  3. Fix: in a stacked / trending regime, take a market entry (accept a slightly worse price) or skip. Reserve pullback-limits for neutral / range regimes. 修法:stacked / 趨勢 regime 就 market 進(接受稍差價位)或跳過。回撤限價留給 neutral / range。

  2. Check regime first: stacked drift (POC ladder aligned) = trade with it; flat / non-stacked = mean-reversion is safe. 先看 regime:stacked drift(POC 梯對齊)= 順勢做;flat / non-stacked = 才適合回歸。
  3. If you must counter-trend, wait for a structural signal (BOS / CHoCH / sweep) confirming the turn — don't fade on RSI alone. 真要逆勢,等結構訊號(BOS / CHoCH / 掃單)確認轉折 — 別只靠 RSI 就 fade。

🪝 Don't put the stop at the invalidate — it's a sweep magnet 止損別貼 invalidate —— 那是掃損磁鐵 ▾
  1. Touch-entry into the pivot zone + stop just past the invalidate = you ARE the liquidity. The invalidate line only breaks structure on a close, but intrabar wicks routinely spear through it to grab stops, then reverse. 樞紐區觸價進 + 止損貼 invalidate = 你就是被掃的流動性。 invalidate 只有收盤破才算結構失效,但盤中影線常常插穿它去吃停損、再反轉。
  2. Fix: enter on close-reclaim confirmation (a bar closes back below/above after tagging the zone — so you're positioned AFTER the sweep), and place the hard stop beyond the sweep high/low (invalidate + buffer / past the swing), not on the line itself. 修法:等收回確認才進(碰過區後一根收回,你就站在 sweep 之後),硬止損放在 sweep 高/低之外(invalidate + 緩衝 / 過 swing),不是貼在線上。
  3. Not "widen the stop" — reposition it. Same direction, same target, only the entry timing + stop location change → the difference between −1R and +4R. 不是「拉寬」,是「換位置」。 同方向同目標,只改進場時機 + 止損位 → 就是 −1R 和 +4R 的差別。

🎯 Trade the edges of a range, never the middle 箱體只做邊,不做中 ▾
  1. Mid-range entry (pos ~50%) has no edge. You're close to BOTH stop and target, so normal chop shakes you out — the "awkward entry" feeling is real, it's a structural problem, not bad luck. 箱中進場(pos ~50%)沒有 edge。 離止損和目標都近,一般洗盤就把你甩出去——那種「尷尬」的感覺是真的,是結構問題不是運氣。
  2. Only act at the edges: long the bottom third, short the top third. Middle = hang a "no-trade" sign and wait. 只在邊緣動手:下緣三分做多、上緣三分做空。中間 = 掛「不交易」牌,等。
  3. Can't reach the edge? Then there's no trade — patience is the position. 摸不到邊?那就沒有單——空手等待也是一種部位。

🔄 Reverse only when a level breaks — not when price wiggles 反手 = 位階作廢才反,不是價格晃 ▾
  1. Frequent reversals = reacting to the last candle. Flipping because price moved 15 pts against you pays spread + fees and never lets a thesis play out. 頻繁反手 = 在反應上一根 K。 因為它逆你走 15 點就翻,付了價差 + 手續費,又不讓任何劇本走完。
  2. Reverse only when your pre-defined invalidation breaks on a close. Write that line down BEFORE you enter — the flip trigger is a level, not a feeling. 只有進場前定好的作廢線被收盤攻破才反手。 進場前就把那條線寫死——反手觸發是「位階」,不是「感覺」。
  3. One regime, one thesis, one side. Don't buy the middle and simultaneously plan to short slightly higher — that's two half-theses colliding. 一個 regime、一個劇本、一邊。 別「中間買多、又計劃小漲一點反手空」——那是兩個半套劇本對撞。

📊 Same session: short → flip long 2463 → plan flip short 2481. Each flip chased the last move instead of a level. Manage the leak: define the flip level up front. 📊 同一場:空 → 反手多 2463 → 又計劃反手空 2481。每次翻都在追上一個動作、不是位階。修法:進場前就定好反手位。

⏱️ Confirm on the setup's timeframe — not a faster one, not the live tick 確認要看「setup 的 TF」收盤 —— 不是更快的、更不是即時跳 ▾
  1. Confirmation TF = the TF the level is drawn on. A 1h EQH/pivot breakout confirms on the 1h close; a 15m setup on the 15m close; a 4h level on the 4h close. Higher TF = stronger but slower. 確認 TF = 那個價位所在的 TF。 1h 的 EQH/樞紐突破 → 看 1h 收盤;15m setup → 15m 收;4h → 4h 收。TF 越高越強但越慢。
  2. Entering on the live tick = zero confirmation = too early. The wick that pokes the level and reverses is the trap; the CLOSE is the truth. 用即時跳進場 = 零確認 = 進太快。 戳一下就反轉的插針是陷阱,收盤才是真相。
  3. Want to enter early? Use the 15m close as a scout entry BUT with a tighter stop, and bail immediately if the setup-TF (1h) close rejects the level. 想早進? 可用 15m 收當前哨,但止損收緊,而且 setup TF(1h)收盤若拒絕該價位就立刻認錯。

🕛 Session opens = institutional anchors: bias filter + S/R + magnet 開盤價 = 機構錨點:偏見過濾 + S/R + 回歸磁鐵 ▾
  1. Bias filter: price above BOTH daily & weekly open → long only; below both → short only. The open is the intraday/intraweek bull-bear divider — algos and desks anchor to it. 偏見過濾:站上日開且週開 → 只做多;都在下 → 只做空。開盤價是當日/當週的多空分界,算法跟機構都錨定它。
  2. S/R reaction: opens act as support/resistance — trade the bounce or rejection off them. A close back above/below an open = character shift (reclaim = bullish). S/R 反應:開盤價當支撐/壓力,做反彈或拒絕。收回站上/跌破開盤價 = 性格轉變(收復=轉多)。
  3. Mean-reversion magnet: when price extends far from an open, it tends to revert and test it — use the open as a TP or a fade reference, not a chase. 回歸磁鐵:價格離開盤價太遠 → 常回測它 → 拿開盤價當 TP 或 fade 參考,別追。
  4. Session framing + clusters: London (07:00 UTC) & NY (13:00 UTC) opens frame each session — watch the "open → sweep → reverse" killzone. When several opens cluster at one price, that's a stronger wall. 場次框架 + 群聚:倫敦(07:00 UTC)/紐約(13:00 UTC)開框各場次,盯「開盤→掃單→反轉」killzone。多條開盤價擠在同一價 = 更強的牆。
