Dashboard legend

Chart lines

  • POC200 (solid strong purple) — long-term Point of Control, ~8 days on 1h. The single price where most volume traded over the lookback.
  • POC100 (medium dashed purple) — ~4-day POC.
  • POC50 (light dashed purple) — recent ~2-day POC; closest to current price.
  • VAH / VAL (dashed purple edges + faint purple band) — Value Area High/Low; the band holding 70% of volume.
  • mark (dashed yellow) — live BingX mark price.
  • entry (solid blue) — engine's planned entry level.
  • stop (solid red) — engine's stop level.
  • TP1 / TP2 (solid green) — 1R / 2R targets.

Meta-row chips

  • POC — primary 200-bar POC price; tap to copy.
  • VA — VAL ↔ VAH copy buttons (the 70% volume zone edges).
  • HVN — top-5 secondary chip zones (high-volume nodes) inside the 200-bar profile.
  • drift ↗ / ↘ / → — POC migration across 50/100/200-bar windows. (POC50 − POC200) / POC200 as %. Rising = bullish regime, falling = bearish, flat = chop. Bold border = stacked (POC50 > POC100 > POC200) = strong confirmation.

Diagnose chips

  • at VAH / at VAL — entry within 0.3% of the value-area edge. Best mean-rev rotation setups (target = POC).
  • inside VA — entry inside [VAL, VAH] but not at an edge. Chop / equilibrium; weak edge either side.
  • above VA / below VA — entry outside the value area. Either acceptance (new regime forming) or extension (reversion candidate).
  • POC ↗ / ↘ — current regime per POC migration.
  • ⚠ falling knife — a big bearish range-expansion bar fired in the last 3 candles. Fighting fresh downward momentum is risky for longs.
  • ⚠ blow-off risk — same but bullish; risky for shorts.

Two scores explained

  • score N (meta row, integer 0–8) — count of independent confluence votes that fired in the engine: RSI extreme, MACD cross, BOLL touch, fib 0.618, sweep, RSI/CVD divergence, range expansion. 3 is the minimum tradeable threshold. It's a "did anything agree at all?" gate.
  • X.X/10 (diagnose row, float 0–10) — validator's structural fit for a specific entry at the current price. Weighted sum of ~20 factors: engine alignment (+2), sweep / fib / HVN proximity (+1.5–2), VA edge (+0.5), POC drift alignment (+0.5–0.7), minus penalties for entry chase, falling knife, counter-regime, inside-VA chop, etc.
  • They don't have to match. A confluent setup (high score) can still be structurally weak (low /10) if it fights regime or is far from value. The /10 is the better single number to act on — it integrates more context.

Verdict colors

  • STRONG — score ≥ 7.5, take it.
  • TAKE — score 5.5–7.5, good setup.
  • NEUTRAL / WEAK — score 3.5–5.5, marginal.
  • AVOID — score < 3.5, skip.

Open-trade card

  • Card border color tracks the trade's current R: green if > 0, red if < 0.
  • The horizontal bar shows position between stop (left, red), entry (center), and TP2 (right, green).
  • Live diagnose re-runs the validator at the trade's own side, not the engine's preferred side — answers "is MY trade still good?", not "what's the best trade now?".
