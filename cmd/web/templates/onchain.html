On-chain — top holders + dump-signal lookup

Discretionary tool — type a ticker (e.g. AKE) or CoinGecko id (e.g. lorenzo-protocol). Returns the top 20 holders and flags any recent transfer to a known CEX hot wallet (last 48h) as a dump signal. Data: CoinGecko + Moralis.

How to interpret this page

Read the two sections top-down

Dump-signals first (the "any red flag right now?" answer), then top-holders for concentration context.

1. Dump signals — how to read each row

ColumnMeaningHow to read
# (rank)which top-holder position#1-3 = strong signal; #15-20 = background noise
→ Exchangewhich CEX receivedsingle exchange = normal; multiple exchanges in same window = coordinated distribution
Amounttokens sentcompare to their total holding: >20% of their balance = clear reduction; <5% = routine
Whentime since transfer<6h = fresh; 24-48h = stale evidence

Strong dump signal (red flag)

  • Top 3 holder dumping >20% of their balance within 6h
  • Multiple top-10 holders hitting CEX in the same 24h window
  • Single transfer > 1-3% of total supply

Weak / noise (usually ignore)

  • Only #15-20 rank moving small amounts
  • Transfer < 1% of that holder's balance (rebalance, not exit)
  • Single isolated transaction, tiny relative to float

2. Top-20 holders — concentration metrics

Top 1 % — single-point risk

> 30%extreme — one dump can crush the market
10-30%manageable but watch closely
< 10%healthy distribution

Top 20 combined — total concentration

> 80%manipulable, ~10 wallets can move the market
50-80%common for low-float alts (AKE / BANK sit here)
< 50%well distributed

Green flags (concentration risk reduced)

  • Top positions labeled as contracts (LP / staking / vesting / burn) — these don't actively dump
  • CEX-labeled rows (red-tinted) — exchange custodial; separate from "whale accumulation"

Red flags (concentration amplified)

  • Multiple unlabeled EOAs in top 5 with similar sizes — possible cluster (one entity in many wallets)
  • Top 1 unlabeled EOA > 20% — single-point risk

Decision frameworks

A. Before going LONG on alt X

  • Any dump signal in last 48h?
    • Top-3 holder hit CEX = strong red flag, skip or halve size
    • Only #15-20 small amounts = tolerable
    • None = no immediate landmine (does NOT confirm bullish edge, just no red flag)
  • Concentration healthy? Top 1 < 15%, top 20 < 70% = OK
  • Bonus: high contract-label coverage (LP + treasury) = trustable float

B. Already holding — when to exit

  • Recheck every 1-2 days:
    • New dump signals appearing AND rank moving forward (#8 → #5 → #3) = whales queuing up to exit
    • Multiple CEX hit simultaneously = accelerating distribution
  • Signal appears → exit. Silent + price rising → hold.

C. Comparing two alt candidates

Technicals similar? Prefer the one WITHOUT dump signals.

Limitations — what this tool CANNOT tell you

The why behind a CEX transfercould be OTC facilitation, staking route, treasury, not just sell prep
Cross-chain flowsbridge out → sell elsewhere is invisible here
OTC / off-chain saleswhale-to-whale direct transfers show but don't tag as dump
Intent (accum vs distrib)we see direction, not motive
Real-timeMoralis free tier lags 5-15 min
Beyond top 20#21+ could be doing worse; we don't see them

Framing

This is a "check for red flags before entering" tool, not a "predict the pump" tool. Absence of dump signals ≠ bullish edge; presence of them ≠ certain top. Feed it into your discretionary judgement alongside price / structure / narrative.

兩個 section 由上而下讀

先看 dump signals（「當下有沒有紅旗」的判斷），再看 top-20 holders 抓集中度風險。

1. Dump signals — 每一列怎麼讀

欄位意義怎麼讀
#（rank）是第幾大戶#1-3 = 強訊號；#15-20 是背景噪音
→ Exchange收貨的 CEX單一 exchange = 常規；多個 exchange 同時收貨 = 協調式分佈
Amount送多少 tokens對照他的總持倉：送 >20% 自己餘額 = 明顯減倉；<5% = 常規操作
When多久以前<6h = 新鮮；24-48h 已是 stale evidence

強 dump signal（紅旗）

  • Top 3 holder 在 6h 內大量出貨（單筆 > 他持倉 20%）
  • 多個 top-10 holder 在同一 24h 窗內同時往 CEX 走
  • 單筆金額 > 全流通供給 1-3%

弱訊號 / 通常忽略

  • 只有 #15-20 這種小持倉戶動作
  • 單筆是他持倉的 <1%（rebalance 不是 exit)
  • 只有一筆、金額對整體流通量微不足道

2. Top-20 holders — 集中度指標

Top 1 佔比 — 單點風險

> 30%極危險 — 一個人 dump 市場撐不住
10-30%可控但要警覺
< 10%健康分佈

Top 20 合計 — 整體集中度

> 80%可操縱、~10 個人可以合力砸盤
50-80%low-float alt 常見區間（AKE / BANK 都在此）
< 50%分散良好

加分項（集中度風險減輕)

  • 前幾名是 labeled contract（LP / staking / vesting / burn）→ 這些不會主動 dump
  • 標 CEX 的（紅底列）→ 是交易所託管、跟「whale accumulation」意義不同

扣分項（集中度風險放大)

  • Top 5 有多個 unlabeled EOA、大小接近 → 可能是同一實體用多地址（cluster 風險)
  • Top 1 是 unlabeled EOA 且 >20% → 單點崩盤風險

決策框架

A. 進 alt X 之前

  • Dump signals section 有沒有東西？
    • Top-3 holder 剛送 CEX = 強紅旗，跳過或減倉
    • 只有 #15-20 小額 = 可接受
    • 完全空 = 無 immediate 紅旗（不等於有 edge，只是沒地雷)
  • 集中度 healthy？Top 1 < 15%、Top 20 < 70% = OK
  • 加分：contract label 覆蓋率高（LP + treasury）= 可信浮動供給

B. 已持倉、要不要退場

  • 每 1-2 天重看：
    • 有沒有新 dump signal、rank 逐次往前（#8 → #5 → #3）= 大戶排隊出貨
    • 多個 CEX 同時被 hit = 分佈加速
  • 訊號出現 → 出。無訊號 + 價穩 → 續持。

C. 兩個候選 alt 比較

技術面類似時，沒有 dump signal 的那個優先。

限制 — 這工具做不到

判斷「為何送 CEX」可能是 OTC 中繼、staking 路由、treasury，不只賣壓準備
追跨鏈動作bridge 出去 → 另一鏈 → CEX：這條 flow 追不到
抓 OTC / 場外大戶 A 直接轉大戶 B、沒經 CEX = 完全隱形
分辨 accumulation vs distribution 的 intent只看方向、不看動機
Real-timeMoralis free tier lag 5-15 min
Top 21+只看前 20；#21 可能正在做動作

Framing

這是「進場前檢查紅旗」的工具，不是「預測 pump」的工具。沒 dump signal 不等於有 bullish edge、有 dump signal 不等於一定見頂。搭配你的價格 / 結構 / 敘事判斷用。
